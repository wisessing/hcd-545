Changes for this course: textures resized to at most 1024 px and stored as WebP, and the geometry compressed with meshopt
(gltf-transform optimize), so that the file is 1.2 MB instead of 10 MB. The shapes and units are unchanged.

Real-world size check (glTF units are metres; bounding box of the file in this folder):

| Width (x) | Height (y) | Depth (z) |
|---|---|---|
| 2.73 m | 1.12 m | 0.92 m |

The sofa sits on the floor (lowest point at y = 0) and is used at its authored size, with no scaling.
